Experimental project

Video publishing with owner control.

GoNews Publisher is a prototype integration that aims to help the operator of TikTok account @g0news1 upload authorized videos through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Development status — not publicly launched

The TikTok Sandbox Login Kit connection has been tested successfully. A protected publishing panel is deployed for the account owner, but TikTok Sandbox does not allow video publishing. The latest supplied TikTok Developers screenshot (October 15, 2026) shows Production: Draft; the live review state has not been independently rechecked. Publishing is disabled by configuration. The app will not claim that a video is published until TikTok confirms it.

Available prototype

Connect TikTok (Sandbox) · Owner publishing panel · Prepare MP4 locally

Planned functionality

After official authorization, the account owner will be able to select a prepared MP4, approve its description and privacy options, submit it through TikTok, and verify the publication status. The app will respect TikTok's content and API limits.

Production eligibility

TikTok currently excludes private/personal apps and upload utilities limited to accounts managed by their owner or team from Direct Post approval. This owner-only prototype has that unresolved eligibility blocker. A successful test login or deployed website does not resolve it.
